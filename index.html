Vita Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

About Vita

Vita is a personal finance application currently intended for private, non-commercial use. It helps the user view and understand their own financial information.

Bank Account Information

Vita may access bank account information through Enable Banking using Open Banking connections authorised by the user.

This information may include:

  • Account information
  • Account balances
  • Transaction history
  • Transaction descriptions and dates

Bank Login Credentials

Vita does not request, receive or store online banking passwords, PINs or other bank login credentials.

Bank authentication takes place directly with the user's bank or its authorised Open Banking interface.

How Financial Data Is Used

Financial information accessed by Vita is used to provide personal finance functionality such as displaying balances and transactions, categorising spending, identifying recurring transactions and providing personal financial insights.

Sharing of Information

Vita does not sell personal or financial information to third parties. Information is only processed as necessary to provide the application's functionality and communicate with the Open Banking provider.

Security

Sensitive API credentials and private cryptographic keys are not embedded in the Vita mobile application. Open Banking API communication is handled through secure server-side infrastructure.

Revoking Access

The user may revoke their bank connection through their bank or the relevant Open Banking provider. Once access is revoked, Vita will no longer be able to retrieve new banking information from that connection.

Data Retention

Financial information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide Vita's personal finance functionality or until the user removes the relevant data or bank connection.

Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, contact the Vita application owner.
